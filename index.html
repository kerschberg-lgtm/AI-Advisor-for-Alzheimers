How this site handles your information

Nothing on this site asks for or accepts genetic data. The APOE explanations work from the result you can already read in your 23andMe account, and the Advisor stores nothing. That is a deliberate choice: in the United States, software that analyses a DNA file and reports Alzheimer's risk is a regulated medical device, and holding genetic data brings obligations under state genetic-privacy laws. An educational site crosses neither line.
