    Plasma phosphorylated tau 217 percentage by race and ethnicity in preclinical Alzheimer disease
    Molina-Henry DP, et al. · 12 October 2026

    Earlier disclosure of blood-based biomarkers, diagnostic certainty, and clinical management: a randomized clinical trial
    Estragués-Gázquez I, et al. · 5 October 2026

    A randomised trial showing that giving clinicians blood-biomarker results earlier improved diagnostic certainty and changed management.

  • JAMA Neurology
    Plasma biomarkers for neocortical tau burden
    Di Molfetta G, et al. · 1 October 2026

    Which blood markers best track the spread of tau into the cortex, the stage most closely tied to symptoms.

  • Journal of the American Medical Association
    Prognostic value of blood-based p-tau217 levels for progression to cognitive impairment
    Buckley RF, et al. · 15 September 2026

    In 2,684 cognitively normal adults, each standard-deviation rise in p-tau217 raised the risk of progression by 38 percent; the highest group had a 38 percent absolute risk over five years.

Landmark studies, 2024 to 2025

  • EHJ
    Lipoprotein(a) and risk of dementia: findings from three cohort studies
    Thomas PE, et al. · European Heart Journal · November 2025

    539,478 people; no continuous association between Lp(a) and dementia, with small isoforms linked to higher Alzheimer's risk.

  • Journal of the American Medical Association
    Structured vs self-guided multidomain lifestyle interventions for global cognitive function: the US POINTER randomized clinical trial
    Baker LD, et al. · August 2025

    2,111 older adults; the structured programme improved cognition more than the self-guided one, in APOE ε4 carriers and non-carriers alike.

  • Nature Medicine
    Plasma proteomics links brain and immune system aging with healthspan and longevity
    Oh HS, et al. · August 2025
  • Nature Communications
    Machine learning in Alzheimer's disease genetics
    Bracher-Smith M, et al. · July 2025

    Machine learning on 41,686 genomes recovered every known locus and found six new ones, but did not beat classical methods for individual prediction.

  • Annals of Neurology
    Vitamin B12 levels association with functional and structural biomarkers of central nervous system injury in older adults
    Beaudry-Richard A, et al. · June 2025

    Lower B12, even within the normal range, was linked to slower nerve conduction and more white-matter damage.

  • Circulation
    Genetic and lifestyle risks for coronary artery disease and long-term risk of incident dementia subtypes
    Sittichokkananon A, et al. · April 2025
  • Alzheimer's Research & Therapy
    Benchmarking Alzheimer's disease prediction: personalised risk assessment using polygenic risk scores
    Bellou E, et al. · 2025

    APOE plus a polygenic score reaches an AUC of 0.72 to 0.76, the realistic ceiling for genetic prediction today.

  • Lancet
    Dementia prevention, intervention, and care: 2024 report of the Lancet standing Commission
    Livingston G, et al. · August 2024

    The fourteen modifiable risk factors, now including high LDL cholesterol and untreated vision loss.
